ACCOUNT DELETION

Delete Your SmartAlphaX Account

You can use this page to request deletion of your SmartAlphaX account and associated personal data. You do not need to reinstall the app to access this page.

Important: Account deletion may be permanent. You may lose your saved learning progress, quiz results, profile information, and other account-associated data.

What data may be deleted?

  • Your SmartAlphaX account and authentication profile.
  • Your name, email address, and account profile data.
  • Saved learning progress and quiz results associated with your account.
  • Account settings and other associated personal data.
  • Locally stored account-specific data, when the app can access the device.

What data may be retained?

Certain records may need to be retained where required by applicable law or for legitimate security purposes. Any retained data should be limited to what is necessary, with the applicable retention period and reason explained.

Shared educational content, such as lessons and general question banks, may remain available because it is not necessarily personal data belonging to an individual user.

Submit an Account Deletion Request

Complete the form below to prepare your deletion request. Your identity must be verified before an account can be deleted.

Never enter your password, OTP, recovery code, or other authentication secrets into this form.

How does deletion work?

  1. Submit a deletion request through the configured secure process.
  2. Verify that you own the account.
  3. The deletion system processes your account and associated data.
  4. You receive a confirmation when deletion is completed.
Processing time

The actual processing period must be specified by the app developer after the backend and deletion workflow have been configured.

Need Help?
